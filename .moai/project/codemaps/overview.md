## Project goal

A web application for a shared hospital touch-screen kiosk. A patient, without sign-up or login, selects the current indemnity-insurance generation, the insurer and required conditions, and treatments, then sees on a separate result screen the total expected payment, the expected insurance payout, and the expected out-of-pocket amount after payout. The estimate comes from a deterministic calculation engine over published hospital prices and source-backed policy rules; generative AI is not used. A hospital administrator manages treatments and prices through a separate PC admin web.
